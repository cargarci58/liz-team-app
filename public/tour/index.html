What it does for you, start to finish

From the first phone call to the money in your account.

🌱 Gets you the next deal
  • Lead generation: intake links, leads flagged to call today
  • A daily call list with follow-ups, picked for you
  • Who's likely to move, before they call someone else
  • Pop-bys planned, note cards written, route mapped
  • Scripts for price reductions, referrals, follow-ups
📄 Does the paperwork
  • Writes offers for you — four answers, official Florida contract
  • Receives offers for you — reads them, parks them, seller compares
  • Listing package filled in one click
  • E-signature with certificate, unsigned requests chased
  • Reads new dates off any addendum and moves the timeline
🤖 Coordinates the transaction by itself
  • Upload a contract — the whole timeline builds itself
  • Reminders to the lender, title, the other agent, your client
  • Communicates with all the parties when a step is done
  • Chases whoever goes quiet until you say stop
  • Morning briefing and a nightly Deal Doctor for every deal
🏠 Takes care of your clients
  • A private portal: where it stands, what's needed, what's next
  • Sellers see and compare offers with their net proceeds
  • Every email sounds like you and is sent in your name
  • Documents shared straight into their portal
💵 Handles your money
  • Replaces QuickBooks: bank import, made / spent / kept
  • Printable P&L, budget vs. actuals, 1099s
  • Commission income from closed deals, automatically
  • Net sheets for sellers, cash-to-close for buyers
📈 Coaches you
  • Growth Plan: your income goal → conversations per day
  • Reports: where people drop off between call and contract
  • Ask-it-anything assistant — type it or say it
  • A one-minute video on every page, whenever you need it

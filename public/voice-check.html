Voice check

Four quick tests to find out why the assistant isn't talking on this computer. Turn your volume up, then work down the page. Each test takes a couple of seconds.

1Your computer's default voice

The plainest possible test — no special voice chosen.

Did you hear it?

2The voice the app picks

The app prefers a Google voice. Those are streamed over the internet, and they can report success while playing nothing at all — this test tells us if that's what's happening.

Did you hear it?

3A built-in Mac voice

A voice installed on your machine, nothing streamed.

Did you hear it?

4Talking while the microphone is on

This is exactly what the assistant does: it opens the microphone, then talks. Your browser may ask for microphone permission — allow it. This is the test that matters most, because it's the one thing your phone never does.

Did you hear it?

5Wake the speech engine back up

Chrome's speech engine can get stuck in a paused state — once it does, every voice on the machine goes silent and stays silent, in every tab, until it's woken up or Chrome is fully quit. That matches exactly what tests 1–4 show. This tries to unstick it.

Did you hear it?

If that didn't do it: fully quit Chrome — Cmd+Q, not just closing the window — then reopen and run test 1 again. That clears the stuck engine in almost every case.

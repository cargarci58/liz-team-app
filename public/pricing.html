Questions, answered

Frequently asked

How does writing an offer in "one click" actually work?

Pick the property and buyer from records you already have. TransactPro assembles the full FAR/BAR offer, fills every field, and flags anything missing. You review, sign, and send — no blank forms, no retyping.

What happens when I receive an offer?

The platform reads the incoming offer, extracts the price, parties, and key terms, and automatically builds a structured transaction with its timeline and party list. You review and approve to go Under Contract. Several offers? Compare them side by side.

Will this really replace my other subscriptions?

For most agents, yes — forms, e-sign, CRM, transaction coordination, bookkeeping, document storage, reminders, and texting all live inside TransactPro. Most users cancel $350+/month in other tools after switching.

Is my clients' data safe?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, documents use secure expiring links, each brokerage's data is fully isolated, and access is role-based. Florida compliance and SMS consent handling are built in.

Do my clients need to download an app?

No. Buyers, sellers, and other parties get a secure link and can view status, confirm dates, and upload documents with one tap — no app, no password.
